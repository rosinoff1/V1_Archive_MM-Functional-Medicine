Meet Your Practitioner

As a Board-Certified Family Nurse Practitioner with extensive experience in functional and integrative medicine, I bring a unique perspective shaped by years in emergency and critical care settings at renowned hospitals across the country.

My background in western medicine created a strong foundation; however, I recognized that the conventional model has limitations when it comes to optimizing health, investigating difficult-to-diagnose symptoms, and preventing chronic conditions.

I was discouraged by the "band-aid" approach and wanted to provide more impactful, personalized care. That's why I now focus on finding the root cause of your health concerns and creating customized treatment plans that truly make a difference.
